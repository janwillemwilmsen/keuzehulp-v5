


Core Functionality
This tool manages questionnaires and provides advice based on the answers.
The owners and contentmanagers can create edit and delete multiple choise and single select and open questions and answers and provide scores for each answer. Like a contentmanagement system.
The owners and contentmanager can create questionnaires for the users of the webapp to fill in, and get an advice from. The advice is calculated based upon scores derived from answers in the questionnaires.
The data is written to a supabase database. 

The main use case is to create questionnaires for energy contract advice. The flow is interactive web-based wizard designed to advise users on the best type of energy contract for their personal situation (e.g., Variable, Fixed 1-year, Fixed 2-year, Fixed 3-year, or Dynamic).
The questionnaires are used for customers of two different energy suppliers. Essent and Energiedirect. Essent does not sell 2 year fixed contracts.

The Wizard Flow: Users click through a sequence of questions regarding their household, flexibility, liking/preference and termination.

The Algorithm: It computes scores and matched percentages for the 4 or 5 different energy contract types.

Results & Feedback: 
At the end, it shows the recommended contract type alongside the percentage match. 
For the supplier Essent it shows the 4 contract types. For the supplier Energiedirect it shows the 5 contract types. Every advice is supported by explanations multi attribute utility theory and feature preference mapping and transparant Recommendations.

Contrastive Explanation. Instead of just saying "This is good for you," the system uses contrastive explanations. It highlights the tension between your preferences. 
Multi-Attribute Utility Theory (MAUT)This is the underlying logic. The "70% match" suggests that the system assigns weights to different attributes (price, duration, sustainability).The "Values": These are the high-scoring attributes where your needs perfectly overlap with the contract's features (the green checkmarks).The "Negative Variants": These represent "Conflict Resolution." The system acknowledges where the product is a "mismatch" for a specific sub-preference but explains why it still fits the overall profile.
Feature-Preference Mapping and transparent Recommendation (Trust Calibration)By showing the "negative" side (the yellow bullets), the tool is performing Trust Calibration. If a tool only shows one match, users often become skeptical.
